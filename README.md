## 🔒 Privacy & Safety Principles

- **No Private Logistics Hardcoded:** Zero data from `12 - Private` or `09 - Final Surprise/Real World Surprise.md` is exposed.
- **Obfuscated Validation:** The secret passcode validation is computed mathematically rather than exposed in clear text.
- **Strictly Separated Placeholders:** Photos, the final letter, and the video player are clearly labeled with tasteful placeholder UI ready for final asset dropping.
