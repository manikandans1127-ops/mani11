Chapter 1: How We Met & The "Do You Need Tablet?" Moment

The first conversation was a little embarrassing... while everyone else was enjoying the rain dance outside the bus, you were sitting inside looking so tired and exhausted. I climbed in, you were the only one in the bus, and I went to my seat and drank water. When I was about to get down, I asked you:

“Hey, why are you looking so sad?”

I was the most shy guy to speak to a girl, but somehow I found the guts to ask (honestly, I was scared to talk to you because you had that attitude girl vibe!). Then you said: “I am on my periods.” I was shocked to hear that, and out of nowhere I blurted out:

“Do you need tablet?” 💊

Literally the most embarrassing moment in my life... You smiled and asked: “Oh, you have tablets for periods pain?” I totally messed it up, you laughed at me, and I ran out as fast as I could... such a funny first meeting!

Later, while I was having fun with my friends, I could sense you watching the whole day from a distance. When the bus stopped at the tea stop on the state border, only me, the driver, and the cleaner were awake outside while everyone else was sleeping. I noticed you come out, and you brought a friend along just to talk to me:

“Hey, take care and careful to your home go.” 🤍

Chapter 2: The First Date, My Secret Notes & The Fork Incident

We were chatting on Snapchat the day before, so intensely that your friends even asked if I loved you, and then you asked me out on a date for the next day. You arrived a bit late with a sad face. As you started opening up and feeling down about your family situation, somehow I reached out and held your hand. For the first time in my entire life, I held a girl's hand — and you held mine back so tight.

Reading My Notes in the Dessert Shop

We were sitting in a dessert shop after our meal. You somehow managed to take my phone and started reading all the secret notes I had written in the past for my future girlfriend, with lines like “find a girl who loves you more than you do and protect her at all cost.” You were amazed seeing all my journals and poetry. I tried grabbing my phone back, but you held my hand so tight that I was happily arrested in joy!

The Proposal with a Fork

When I first proposed to you, you didn't reject me or say yes immediately — you told me “I need time.” I gave you every single reason why I love you. Then, just to tease you, I said:

“Tell me your decision quickly, I have other jobs to do! My friend Kaviya told me she will find another girl for me...”

I even dialed Kaviya and said: “She is not saying yes, let's find someone else.” All of a sudden, you picked up a fork from the table, held it right to my neck and said:

“Come again?” 😂🍴

I immediately hung up the call and laughed! My hands wouldn't stop shaking after you held them.
